Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

1. Overview

Vaulted Clipsss Uploader ("the App") is a personal tool used only by the owner of the TikTok account @vaultedclipssss. It has no other users and does not collect data from the public.

2. Information the App accesses

When the owner connects their own TikTok account, the App receives, through TikTok's official API:

  • Basic account information (such as display name and profile picture)
  • Permission to upload videos to the owner's account as drafts
  • A list of the owner's own posted videos, to find links to their posts

3. How it is used and stored

This information is used only to upload the owner's videos to the owner's own account. Access tokens and data are stored locally on the owner's own computer. They are not sent to any other server.

4. Sharing

No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties.

5. Deleting data

The owner can disconnect the App at any time in TikTok's settings, which revokes its access, and can delete the locally stored data at any time.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated at any time. The date at the top shows the latest version.

8. Contact
